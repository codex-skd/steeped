<h2>&#10024; Overview</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="65%">
<p>Steeped is a small, cosy mod about tea. Find tea trees growing in the wild, pick their leaves, fire a teapot and a few cups in a kiln, fill the pot with water, boil it, and brew something warm. Every drink does something a little different.</p>

<p>It is a NeoForge 1.21.1 port of <strong>Simply Tea</strong>, the original mod by <strong>Elucent</strong> (who also drew most of its textures) and <strong>KnightMiner</strong>. This port is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original authors.</p>

<h2>&#127919; Features</h2>

<h3>&#127795; Tea trees in the wild</h3>
<p>Tea trees generate in the world. Their leaves are the start of everything: dry them, ferment them, or use them fresh. There are saplings, trunks, and matching fences and fence gates for building with.</p>

<h3>&#129753; Teapots</h3>
<p>Fire an unfired teapot in a kiln, fill it with water, and heat it over a fire to bring it to a boil. A teapot can also hold milk, and frothed milk for the drinks that call for it.</p>

<h3>&#9749; A cupboard of brews</h3>
<p>Green tea, black tea, dandelion tea, chamomile tea, chai, chorus tea, apple iced tea and hot chocolate. Add honey or cinnamon to some of them. Tea bags let you carry a brew with you.</p>

<h3>&#9889; Five effects of their own</h3>
<p>Restful, Relaxed, Caffeinated, Invigorated and Enderfalling. Different drinks give different effects &mdash; pick the cup for the job.</p>

<h3>&#129482; Ice cubes and chorus petals</h3>
<p>Ice cubes and chorus petals turn up as loot and are what make the iced and chorus brews possible.</p>

<h3>&#127760; Configurable</h3>
<p>Drinks, the teapot and tree generation can be tuned from the config file.</p>

<h2>&#128218; How to use</h2>
<ol>
<li>Find a tea tree and collect its leaves.</li>
<li>Craft an unfired teapot and cups, and fire them in a kiln.</li>
<li>Fill the teapot with water and heat it until it boils.</li>
<li>Brew your drink and enjoy the effect.</li>
</ol>

<h2>&#128173; Notes</h2>

<ul>
<li>Steeped is a <strong>port</strong>: the gameplay comes from Simply Tea 2.7.0 by Elucent and KnightMiner (MIT). Their credits are kept in the mod and the source.</li>
<li>It uses its own id, <code>steeped</code>, so it does not conflict with the original. Recipes and datapacks written for the original are not compatible with it.</li>
<li>Nothing is bundled from other mods; there are no dependencies besides NeoForge.</li>
</ul>
